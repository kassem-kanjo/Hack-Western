design/v2/02-baltic.html
  1. One question: what is happening right now? The only large number on screen is 8, top-left of the map. It answers the question before anyone reads a word.
  2. Coverage sits under the headline. A silence only means something if we were listening, so "receivers hearing 591 ships/min" (real: Baltic probe, F5) is part of the first read, with a 24 h sparkline. It turns amber if it drops.
  3. Ships are quiet, events are loud. Thousands of ships are one dim teal field (2.6 px, 55% opacity). Event markers are the only strong shapes on the map.
  4. Density-aware. Where dots would merge (Gdansk anchorage, Gulf of Finland) they collapse into one counted bubble; zooming in splits it back into dots. Never more than ~2,000 individual dots at once.
  5. Kind = shape, family = colour. Silence: amber ring with dashed tail (went quiet). Jump and clone: white triangle / twin squares (position or identity cannot be true). Meeting: pink double ring. Works in greyscale.
  6. The legend is the filter. The kind chips above the list explain every marker and filter it. No separate legend box on this map.
  7. Only the selected event is labelled on the map. The other seven are just numbered markers, so the picture stays calm.
  8. Ranked list, five rows, plain sentences. Each row is one sentence a non-expert reads at 1 m, never a field dump. Rank number links row and marker. Red is used once: a sanctions-watchlist hit.
  9. Time strip. Events as ticks on the last 24 h, hatched = a stretch where our recorder had a hole (we say so). V1: ticks and a "Replay" button. V2: drag to scrub.
  10. Mode badge in the same slot as every screen. LIVE green and pulsing only while the stream is fresh; REPLAY and SNAPSHOT use the violet-white. Attribution bottom-left, always.
