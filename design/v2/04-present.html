Presentation mode

design/v2/04-present.html
  1. One caption, bottom-left, always the same place. One 44 px sentence the room reads, one 20 px line under it. The presenter says the script; the screen says the point.
  2. The live view stays alive behind it. Same screens, same data, scrim only where the caption sits. The presenter can still click. Steps are data (route, focus, caption), so reordering to the v3 Baltic-first story is an edit, not a rebuild.
  3. Keys, shown once and quietly. Right / left to move, Esc to leave. Bottom-right, 13 px, never over the view. P enters, from any screen.
  4. Segmented progress bar across the top edge: where we are in 7 steps with no number to read. The tiny "Step 1 of 7" and "Next: ..." are for the presenter.
  5. Chrome is removed. No nav, no rail, no filters; only the mode badge stays, so the audience always knows this is a snapshot, not live. Funnel numbers light up as each step reaches them (step 1: only 5,624).
  6. Each step keeps its own action above the caption. Step 3 keeps the timeline scrubber at y 640; the caption zone starts at 610 below it. Rule for step views: nothing interactive below 610 px.
  7. Step 3 shows the verdict as a picture: the radar diamond sits outside the violet zone, circled once in white. The word "Cleared" is the caption; the reason is the timing line.
Steps follow docs/demo-script.md (v0). The v3 draft in docs/plan.md opens on the Baltic instead; only the step list changes.
