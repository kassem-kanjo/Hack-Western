Navigation

design/v2/01-nav.html
  1. Brand = home. It returns to the last place you were in. No separate Home tab.
  2. Three places, one bar. Global (the 5,624 → 19 investigation), Baltic (our own live recording), Edge unit (the camera that sees what AIS does not). Five tabs become three. Current place: underline plus brighter text, not colour.
  3. Present sits next to the mode badge, outside the three places, because it is a way of showing the app, not a place in it. Always one click, always P.
  4. Mode badge, one slot, always. Judges must never wonder whether they see live data or a file. Same pill shape, five states; amber and red only when something is wrong.
  5. Depth is a breadcrumb, not more tabs. Event, case and ship pages add a one-line row under the bar; the bar itself never changes, so nobody gets lost. Esc goes back one level.
  6. Controls live where they act. Region and period leave the bar; the Global screen carries them. The bar stays app-level: where, present, status.
  7. Sizes: bar 48 px, labels 13 px, badge 12 px mono, tab hit area 48 px tall. Everything passes the existing AA pairs in tokens.css; nothing new in the palette.
