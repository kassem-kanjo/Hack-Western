# Submission checklist (Hack Western 13)

Source: hack-western-13.devpost.com (overview + rules), read Sun 22 Nov ~00:00 ET.

## Deadlines
- **Devpost locks Sun 22 Nov, 9:00 AM ET.** No edits after. We aim for 8:30.
- **Demo fair: 12:00-1:00 PM.** Three general judges, each gets **3 min demo + 1 min questions**. No slide decks. Table number on the live site. Stay until all three have seen us.
- Sponsor judging (QNX, MLH tracks) is separate: shortlisted teams get a time and room on the live site / email.

## What Kassem submits on Devpost (only he can: his account)
1. Project name, tagline, write-up (draft: docs/devpost-v2-draft.md; add "Accomplishments").
2. Team members added to the project.
3. "Built with" tags (in the draft).
4. Links: GitHub repo (public), demo video (YouTube or Vimeo, uploaded by Kassem).
5. Images: 3-5 screenshots (we make them; no camera frames with people).
6. **Tracks, selected on BOTH Devpost and the Hack Western live site; they must match:**
   - Best Use of QNX (runs on QNX, uses a QNX open-source AI module: OpenCV DNN + YOLOX-nano)
   - [MLH] Best Use of Tiger Data
   - Organizers' Choice: Most Adventurous (no separate entry may be needed; select it if listed)
   - Overall is automatic.
   - Not entering: Vultr (parked), Entire, Gemini, TinyFish, others.

## What we prepare before 8:00
- Video (60-90 s), voice-over (docs/voiceover.md), screenshots.
- README that sells in 10 seconds.
- Final data refresh; app frozen; Pi charged and on the table network.

## At the table (3 min per judge)
- One story, not a tour of screens: one ship goes dark, who it could have met, the sanctions match, the satellite pass, the Pi lookout. Then the Hormuz scale in one line.
- If something fails live, say what we tried and what we found (the rules ask for that).
